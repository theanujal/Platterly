# suppliers

Chunk 18.2 — a Supplier record per kitchen (name unique ignoring case). Inventory items point at it with `supplierId` (the old free-text name and contact columns were migrated into Supplier rows and dropped). Expenses and repeating expenses can point at it too; they also keep `supplierName`, which is copied from the supplier when one is picked and stands alone for a typed one-off shop. Renaming a supplier updates the expenses that point at it. Deleting is refused while anything uses it; deactivate instead. `/suppliers` lists and edits them; `/suppliers/[id]` shows the items they supply and the expenses booked against them. Outstanding balance and purchase orders arrive with 18.3.

## Products Supplied (2026-10-15)

`SupplierItem` maps a supplier to the inventory items it provides (many to many, unique per supplier and item) with an `isPreferred` flag. At most one supplier per item is preferred: `applyProducts` clears the flag on the other supplier inside the same transaction, and a partial unique index (`supplier_item_one_preferred_per_inventory`, in the migration only) is the backstop for two simultaneous saves. `createSupplier` / `updateSupplier` take an optional product list: undefined leaves the mappings alone, an empty list removes them all. Items must belong to the same kitchen (`assertProducts`). A mapping is not history, so it goes with its supplier or item; deleting a supplier is still refused only when inventory (`supplierId`), expenses, orders or payments use it. `Inventory.supplierId`, Purchasing and Receiving are unchanged. Inventory has no active/inactive flag, so every item is offered.

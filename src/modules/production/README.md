# production

Chunk 18.4 — what orders need from the store. `production.ts` turns an order's meal plan into ingredient quantities through each dish's recipe, cooked for the guests plus the Kitchen Rules' extra percentage (the same rule as the prep sheet), against stock on hand. `takeOrderStock` is the confirmed stock take: it runs once, only while the order is Sent to Kitchen, after a person has seen the needs and shortfalls. Where stock is short it takes what is there (never below zero) and writes the shortfall on the ledger row. Cancelling or editing the order afterwards does not put stock back; correct it with a Stock In or Adjustment. `getProductionPlan` is the Production Planning page (`/kitchen-dashboard/production`): orders with the kitchen in the board's window and the combined shortfall.

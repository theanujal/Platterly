This policy explains how Fragen Network Private Limited ("Platterly", "we", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on platterly.in and in Caterly by Platterly at catering.platterly.in. It should be read with our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).

## 1. In short

- This marketing website (platterly.in) uses **analytics and marketing cookies only if you accept them**. Analytics tools count visits and show which pages are useful; marketing tools help us measure and reach people interested in Platterly. Each stays off until you choose Accept, and the tools in use are listed in the cookie notice and in the table at the end of this page.
- You can change your choice at any time with **Cookie settings** in the footer of this website.
- The Caterly by Platterly app uses **only the cookies it needs to work**: to keep you signed in, to keep your account secure and to remember simple choices.
- You can block or delete cookies in your browser at any time. If you block the essential ones, you will not be able to sign in.

## 2. What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, do the same job. Some are needed for a website to work; others measure how it is used or show advertising.

## 3. What we use on this website

| What | Purpose | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Local storage entry for a closed announcement | Remembers that you closed the bar at the top of the page, so it does not come back on every page | Until you clear your browser data |
| Local storage entry for your cookie choice | Remembers whether you accepted or declined analytics and marketing, so we do not ask on every page | Until you clear your browser data |
| Cookies of the analytics and marketing tools you accept | Set only after you accept; each tool, what it does and the cookies it sets are listed in the table at the end of this page | Set by the tool, often up to 2 years |

Fonts and images are served from our own domain, so this website does not send your visit to a font or image provider. Analytics and marketing on this website are off until you accept them in the cookie notice. If you decline, or never answer, no such cookie is set and nothing about your visit is sent to those tools. If you accept, the tool's provider receives your visit data (such as the pages you view, how you arrived, your browser and device type, and your approximate location) and may process it on servers outside India. Some tools, such as session-replay tools, also record how you move through a page. These tools are used only on platterly.in, never inside Caterly by Platterly.

## 4. What we use in Caterly by Platterly

| Cookie type | Purpose | Can you turn it off? |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-in session | Keeps you signed in as you move between pages. It is secure, cannot be read by scripts on the page, and expires | No. Without it you cannot use your account |
| Security | Helps protect your account from forged requests and misuse | No |
| Preferences | Remembers simple choices, such as whether the sidebar is open | Yes. The app still works, but forgets the choice |

## 5. Third parties

When you pay for a plan, you are taken to our payment provider, Razorpay, whose checkout may set its own cookies to process the payment and prevent fraud. We do not control those cookies; please read [Razorpay's privacy policy](https://razorpay.com/privacy/). If you accept analytics or marketing on this website, the providers of the tools listed at the end of this page set their own cookies; please read each provider's privacy policy. We do not use third-party advertising networks.

## 6. How to control cookies

Every major browser lets you see, block and delete cookies: look for "Cookies" or "Site data" in the privacy settings of Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge. Blocking all cookies will stop sign-in from working. To change your analytics choice, use **Cookie settings** in the footer of this website. To clear the announcement and cookie-choice entries, clear site data for platterly.in.

## 7. Changes

If we change how we use cookies, we will update this page and its date. A change that needs your consent will ask for it first, and if you accepted an earlier version we will ask again.
